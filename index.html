About Hyara

A studio where every mirror believes in you

Born in Indore and now shining in Mumbai too, Hyara is a luxury unisex salon built around one idea — self-care is not vanity, it's respect.

From precision hair artistry and high-fashion colour to glowing skin rituals and masterful grooming for men, our certified artists blend classic craft with modern technique — always with premium products, hygienic spaces and zero compromise.

Ask us anything
Do I need to book an appointment?+

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking ahead — especially on weekends and for bridal or grooming packages — so you never wait.

Are your products safe for all hair & skin types?+

Yes. We use dermat-tested, paraben-free luxury lines and always run a patch test before colour or chemical treatments.

Do you offer bridal & groom packages?+

Absolutely — our signature bridal and groom rituals include trials, HD make-up / styling, nails and on-the-day support for the full family.

What is the Hyara Academy?+

A hands-on pro course in hair, make-up and grooming taught by our working artists, with live practice and placement support on completion.

Can couples/parents book together?+

Yes — private duo stations are available for couples, friends and families. Book two slots in one form and we'll seat you together.
